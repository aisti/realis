# Realis Team 2 notes
## Session 1: ETYM
2026/07/04

We board the mail carrier's Orphan Vessel "Queen of Peace" and set out for [Etym](./universe.md#etym), on the outskirts of [Suzerainty](./universe.md#the-leo-suzerainty) territory.  The journey will take 3 days.  We get to know one another as Caprice gets their hands in every metaphorical cookie jar aboard.

As we reach the moon, [the Farmers Guild](./universe.md#the-farmers-guild) hails and promptly breaches our Orphan Vessel in order to sap energy.  A massive, diamond-hard acorn lodges in the hull and periodically regrows roots and vines into the bulkheads, seeking always the power core. Caprice prunes it back from taking over the ship, and Coyote tries to send the light of [the Drift](./universe.md#) back to [the First Root](./universe.md#), the great tree that grows from [Realis](./universe.md#).  Contact overwhelms it, sapping its energy and satisfying the pirates.

Etym: Liquid metal, organic shapes, flowing.  Pony finds us a secluded cavern to use as base of operations after checking in with the local post office.  The whole moon is blockaded by Farmer biotech.  Last's gonne draws her to their base and we scare off their guards before breaking in.  

We work together to reverse the jammer and use it to report in to [Lord Crownguard](./universe.md#), informing him of the problem with the Farmers Guild and requesting backup.  The Suzerainty comes in to establish order.

## Session 2: ~~THE STRING OF PEARLS~~ HEIST ON ORIONELLE
2026/07/11

We spend downtime on [Etym](./universe.md#etym) trying to repair the damage to the Queen of Peace.

Tasked with securing an alliance with House Fyonn of [the String of Pearls](./universe.md#the-string-of-pearls) before [the Sphenic Polytechnic](./universe.md#) can beat us to it, we call on an old flame(?) of Caprice's, [Tessa Fyonn](./universe.md#tessa-fyonn), and entice her with a night of excitement pulling a heist at a fashion auction.

The plan is going off without a hitch and everyone is safely infiltrated thanks to Colton. Coyote convinces a model to create space for Tessa in the show when Last's instincts take over and kill her in cold blood in front of everyone.  Panicking under spectral searchlights, Coyote pulls them both into [the Drift](./universe.md#) where they are set upon by creature of the Drift [ATROS, UNSPOKEN CRUELTY](./universe.md#atros-unspoken-cruelty), who drives it to make Last question whether they truly exist as a person. 

Tessa snags the model's dress as it flutters to the floor.  She is decidedly not into Caprice, her heart set on a commoner whom she has decided to neg at a distance.  

We have not succeeded.  However, we HAVE secured... well, maybe a favor.  Blackmail, perhaps?  An ally for future escapades?  We haven't *truly* surprised [Daddy Crownguard](./universe.md#) one way or the other.

## Session 3: NAROVI
2026/08/01

We secure a base of operations on the [String of Pearls](./universe.md#the-string-of-pearls).  During a newscast about [the murder](./universe.md#session-2-the-string-of-pearls-heist-on-orionelle) the [Crownguards](./universe.md#) call to dress Last down, calling into question whether they can keep the heat off us.  We are assigned to establish contact and win over a [Sphenic](./universe.md#) scientist who is considering secretly turning coat.

[Arlix Pearford](./universe.md#) is a young up-and-coming university student in robotics on the unaffiliated moon of Narovi. Caprice and Colton manufacture a defence conference/gun show on Narovi, hiring gyrovagues from Etym and showing off Last as a centrepiece.  A couple dozen attendees show up and are very interested in Last/gonne.

Arlix looks nervous; he has secret funding and is only here because his advisor made him attend. Coyote lures him to a secondary location, with a rookie tail who is in turn tailed by (rookie) Caprice.

As they head to a pub, Last is approached by a reporter of the Participant Pearl for an interview and deftly deflects.  Arlix is getting secret funding from the Kiliad Consortium. Meanwhile Caprice pulls a knife on the tail and attempts to draw some information out of him.  Stalemate: nothing happens.

Coyote starts to pull him into the Drift, and the tail - who turns out to be a spotter - calls in a sniper shot on Arlix.  As Caprice holds the spotter at knifepoint, Colton quickly locates them on the Orphan Vessel's Drift monitors and catches the two as they fall in.  As he bleeds out, Coyote and Colton work together to turn him into a ghost and bind him to the ship.  Although it's succeeding, the vines of the First Root are pulling toward his spirit... but the Signal Seed's leaves protect him.  And so... he is bound to the ship.

Last's gonne pulls them to do better than the sniper.  They veil threats to turn away a reporter, to no avail, attracting even more Participant curiosity.  The sniper evades them as Caprice interrogates the rookie, but they try and goad him out by getting into the open.  Colton picks up Caprice and as we hover, we see them being deliberately unsubtle.

The sniper takes the bait, but is no match for the Last Thing You Never Hear's focus on getting that paper.  They shoot the bullet out of the air, following it up with another shot that takes the sniper out.  Mission... something.
